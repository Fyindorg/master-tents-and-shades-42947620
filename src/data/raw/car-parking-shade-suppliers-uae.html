What We Engineer Around

Specifications We Assess for Every Car Parking Shade

Every design above is engineered against the same checklist, so the shade performs to spec regardless of which style you choose.

Wind Load Capacity

Rated to withstand UAE gusts and storm conditions without excessive fabric flex or frame stress.

Weight Load

Frame and footing sized to carry the fabric, fittings, and – where fitted – solar panel weight.

Entry / Clearance Height

Set to comfortably clear SUVs, vans and trucks without restricting access to the bay.

Fabric Type

HDPE, PVC or PVDF membrane, chosen to match the airflow, waterproofing and finish the site needs.

Fabric Weight (GSM)

Heavier grams-per-square-metre ratings for a stiffer canopy that holds shape and resists tearing longer.

Frame Material

Hot-dip galvanized steel or powder-coated aluminium, matched to span size and coastal exposure.

UV Protection

Fabric rated to block a high share of UV radiation, protecting paint, interiors and skin underneath.

Tilt and Rotation

Adjustable canopy angles on umbrella and select cantilever types to track the sun through the day.

Opening Mechanism

Manual crank or motorised retractable options for umbrella and select sail-type shades.

Vented Canopies

Top venting built into the pitch to let hot air escape instead of pooling as a heat pocket.

Foundation Requirements

Footing depth and ballast calculated per design – cantilever posts especially need uplift-resistant footings.

Solar Integrations

Optional PV panel mounting on flat and pyramid roof types, adding power generation to the shade.
